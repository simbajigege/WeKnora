# National Investment Aspirations / 国家投资愿景

## Definition / 定义

中文：国家投资愿景（NIA）是 [[entity/new-incentive-framework|新激励框架 / New Incentive Framework]] 的核心政策基础之一。文件把 NIA 作为评估投资项目是否符合国家优先事项的参照。

English: National Investment Aspirations (NIA) is one of the core policy foundations of the [[entity/new-incentive-framework|New Incentive Framework / 新激励框架]]. The document uses NIA as a reference for judging whether an investment project aligns with national priorities.

## Link To Scorecard / 与评分卡的关系

中文：[[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA 评分卡 / NIA Scorecard]] 把 NIA 的政策方向转化为制造业项目评估指标。它评估企业承诺和项目能否产生预期成果。

English: The [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA Scorecard / NIA 评分卡]] translates NIA policy directions into evaluation indicators for manufacturing projects. It assesses company commitments and the project's ability to deliver desired outcomes.
