# Malaysian Investment Development Authority / 马来西亚投资发展局

## Definition / 定义

中文：MIDA 是文件中负责 NIF 制造业激励申请与管理的机构。企业通过 InvestMalaysia 平台提交申请，不完整申请会被退回。

English: MIDA is the authority named in the document for receiving and administering applications for NIF manufacturing incentives. Companies submit applications through the InvestMalaysia platform, and incomplete applications are returned.

## Role In Approval / 审批角色

中文：项目经过 [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA 评分卡 / NIA Scorecard]] 评估后，MIDA 会向获批企业发出原则批准函。该函说明获批税收激励、分层水平，以及企业在激励期内必须遵守的条件。

English: After evaluation through the [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA Scorecard / NIA 评分卡]], MIDA issues a principle approval letter to approved companies. The letter states the approved incentive, tiering level, and conditions to be met during the incentive period.

## Role In Compliance / 合规角色

中文：对于 [[concept/special-tax-rate|特别税率 / Special Tax Rate]]，企业必须在每个课税年度结束后七个月内向 MIDA 提交年度合规报告。对于 [[concept/investment-tax-allowance|投资税务津贴 / Investment Tax Allowance]]，企业必须在每个五年 ITA 周期结束后七个月内提交经外部审计师验证的合规声明。

English: For [[concept/special-tax-rate|Special Tax Rate / 特别税率]], companies must submit Annual Compliance Reports to MIDA within seven months after each year of assessment. For [[concept/investment-tax-allowance|Investment Tax Allowance / 投资税务津贴]], companies must submit externally verified compliance declarations within seven months after each five-year ITA period.
