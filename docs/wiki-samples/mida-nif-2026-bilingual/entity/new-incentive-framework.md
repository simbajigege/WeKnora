# New Incentive Framework / 新激励框架

## Definition / 定义

中文：新激励框架（NIF）是马来西亚在 2026 年国家预算中引入的分阶段激励框架。它将制造业激励从单纯的资格式优惠，转向“分层 + 结果导向”的政策工具。

English: The New Incentive Framework (NIF) is a phased incentive framework introduced through Malaysia's National Budget 2026. It moves manufacturing incentives from a simple eligibility-based benefit toward a tiered and outcome-based policy instrument.

## Policy Alignment / 政策对齐

中文：NIF 由两个国家战略支撑：[[entity/national-investment-aspirations|国家投资愿景 / National Investment Aspirations]] 和 [[entity/new-industrial-master-plan-2030|新工业大蓝图 2030 / New Industrial Master Plan 2030]]。它的目标是吸引高增长、高价值投资，并提升经济韧性。

English: NIF is guided by two national strategies: [[entity/national-investment-aspirations|National Investment Aspirations / 国家投资愿景]] and [[entity/new-industrial-master-plan-2030|New Industrial Master Plan 2030 / 新工业大蓝图 2030]]. Its goal is to attract high-growth, high-value investments and strengthen economic resilience.

## How It Works / 运作方式

中文：NIF 为制造业新投资提供两个互斥激励：[[concept/special-tax-rate|特别税率 / Special Tax Rate]] 和 [[concept/investment-tax-allowance|投资税务津贴 / Investment Tax Allowance]]。项目通过 [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA 评分卡 / NIA Scorecard]] 评估，再通过 [[concept/tiering-approach|分层机制 / Tiering Approach]] 对应不同激励待遇。

English: For new manufacturing investments, NIF provides two mutually exclusive incentives: [[concept/special-tax-rate|Special Tax Rate / 特别税率]] and [[concept/investment-tax-allowance|Investment Tax Allowance / 投资税务津贴]]. Projects are assessed using the [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA Scorecard / NIA 评分卡]], and the [[concept/tiering-approach|Tiering Approach / 分层机制]] maps performance to incentive treatment.

## Teaching Notes / 教学提示

中文：讲授时可以把 NIF 看成“政策目标如何转化为企业行为约束”的案例。它不是单纯减税，而是把减税与产业升级、就业质量、本地供应链和可持续发展绑定。

English: For teaching, NIF can be treated as a case of translating policy goals into firm-level behavioral commitments. It is not only tax relief; it ties tax relief to industrial upgrading, job quality, domestic linkages, and sustainability.
