# New Industrial Master Plan 2030 / 新工业大蓝图 2030

## Definition / 定义

中文：新工业大蓝图 2030（NIMP 2030）是马来西亚工业政策蓝图，也是 [[entity/new-incentive-framework|新激励框架 / New Incentive Framework]] 的指导战略之一。

English: The New Industrial Master Plan 2030 (NIMP 2030) is a Malaysian industrial policy blueprint and one of the guiding strategies for the [[entity/new-incentive-framework|New Incentive Framework / 新激励框架]].

## Role In The Guidelines / 在指南中的作用

中文：文件把 NIMP 2030 与 [[entity/national-investment-aspirations|国家投资愿景 / National Investment Aspirations]] 一起作为判断项目战略价值的政策背景。[[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA 评分卡 / NIA Scorecard]] 中也包含“产品是否属于 NIA 目标领域或 NIMP 2030 核心领域”的指标。

English: The document uses NIMP 2030 together with [[entity/national-investment-aspirations|National Investment Aspirations / 国家投资愿景]] as policy context for assessing strategic project value. The [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA Scorecard / NIA 评分卡]] includes an indicator for whether the product is within NIA targeted sectors or NIMP 2030 core sectors.

## Teaching Notes / 教学提示

中文：NIMP 2030 在这个案例中不是执行流程，而是“产业方向筛选器”。学生可以用它理解为什么同样是制造业投资，不同行业、不同技术含量、不同产业链位置会得到不同政策评价。

English: In this case, NIMP 2030 is not an operational process but an industrial direction filter. Students can use it to understand why manufacturing projects receive different policy evaluations depending on sector, technology level, and supply-chain position.
