# Global Minimum Tax / 全球最低税

## Definition / 定义

中文：全球最低税（GMT）在文件中作为国际税收背景出现。它与 OECD/G20 包容性框架下的第二支柱和 GloBE 规则相关，目标是设置 15% 的全球最低有效税率。

English: Global Minimum Tax (GMT) appears in the document as international tax context. It relates to Pillar Two and the GloBE Rules under the OECD/G20 Inclusive Framework, which set a 15% global minimum effective tax rate.

## Malaysia Implementation / 马来西亚实施情况

中文：文件说明马来西亚自 2025 年起实施 GMT。全球年收入至少 7.5 亿欧元的企业集团，如果其在马来西亚的有效税率低于 15%，可能需要缴纳补足税。该补足税可通过《1967 年所得税法》第 XI 部分下的 Domestic Top Up Tax 机制征收，执行日期为 2025 年 1 月 1 日。

English: The document states that Malaysia implements GMT from 2025. Groups with annual global revenue of at least EUR 750 million may be subject to top-up tax if their effective tax rate in Malaysia is below 15%. This top-up tax can be collected through the Domestic Top Up Tax mechanism under Part XI of the Income Tax Act 1967, enforced on 1 January 2025.

## Why It Matters For NIF / 为什么与 NIF 有关

中文：对于大型跨国企业，税收激励可能受到全球最低税规则影响。即使当地提供优惠税率，如果集团整体或辖区有效税率低于 15%，也可能触发补足税。因此，投资激励政策需要和国际税收规则一起理解。

English: For large multinational groups, tax incentives may interact with global minimum tax rules. Even if a local jurisdiction offers a preferential rate, a top-up tax may arise if the group or jurisdictional effective tax rate falls below 15%. Therefore, investment incentives must be understood together with international tax rules.

## Teaching Notes / 教学提示

中文：GMT 可以作为“国内产业政策与国际税收协调”的讨论点。学生可以思考：当全球最低税存在时，东道国应如何设计仍然有效的投资激励？

English: GMT can be used to discuss the coordination between domestic industrial policy and international tax rules. Students can ask: when a global minimum tax exists, how should a host country design investment incentives that still work?
