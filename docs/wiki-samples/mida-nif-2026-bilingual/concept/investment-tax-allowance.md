# Investment Tax Allowance / 投资税务津贴

## Definition / 定义

中文：投资税务津贴（ITA）是 [[entity/new-incentive-framework|NIF / 新激励框架]] 下以资本支出为基础的激励。它允许企业用符合条件的资本支出抵扣一定比例的法定收入。

English: Investment Tax Allowance (ITA) is a capital expenditure-based incentive under [[entity/new-incentive-framework|NIF / 新激励框架]]. It allows a company to offset a percentage of qualifying capital expenditure against statutory income.

## Incentive Range / 激励范围

中文：对制造业新投资，指南说明 ITA 最高可达 100%，期限最长 15 年。该津贴可用于抵扣 70% 至 100% 的法定收入。未使用津贴可结转至以后年度，直到完全使用。

English: For new manufacturing investment, the guidelines state that ITA may be up to 100% for a period of up to 15 years. The allowance can offset between 70% and 100% of statutory income. Unutilised allowance can be carried forward until fully utilised.

## Compliance / 合规

中文：企业必须在运营开始前向 [[entity/malaysian-investment-development-authority|MIDA / 马来西亚投资发展局]] 申请。企业还必须在原则批准函日期后 36 个月内，提交税收激励开始日期确定申请和最低条件合规声明。每个五年 ITA 周期结束后七个月内，还要提交经外部审计师验证的合规声明。

English: The company must apply to [[entity/malaysian-investment-development-authority|MIDA / 马来西亚投资发展局]] before commencing operations. It must also submit an application for determining the tax incentive commencement date and a minimum-condition compliance declaration within 36 months from the principle approval letter. Within seven months after each five-year ITA period, it must submit an externally verified compliance declaration.

## Teaching Notes / 教学提示

中文：ITA 适合用来讲“资本支出型激励”。它鼓励企业把资金投入固定资产、设备和项目建设，但能否持续享受激励仍然取决于履约。

English: ITA is useful for teaching capital expenditure-based incentives. It encourages firms to invest in fixed assets, equipment, and project construction, while continued benefits still depend on compliance.
