# Tiering Approach / 分层机制

## Definition / 定义

中文：分层机制是 [[entity/new-incentive-framework|NIF / 新激励框架]] 中把项目质量、企业承诺和税收激励待遇连接起来的机制。项目通过 [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA 评分卡 / NIA Scorecard]] 得到评价，评价结果影响激励力度。

English: The tiering approach is the mechanism in [[entity/new-incentive-framework|NIF / 新激励框架]] that links project quality, company commitments, and tax incentive treatment. Projects are evaluated through the [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA Scorecard / NIA 评分卡]], and the result influences the incentive quantum.

## Tier 1 And Tier 2 / Tier 1 与 Tier 2

中文：在指南中，最低条件与附加条件决定企业实际可享受的层级。只满足最低条件通常对应 Tier 2；同时满足最低条件和附加条件则对应 Tier 1。若最低条件都未达成，则相应期间无法享受激励。

English: In the guidelines, minimum and additional conditions determine the actual tier enjoyed by the company. Meeting minimum conditions generally gives Tier 2 treatment; meeting both minimum and additional conditions gives Tier 1 treatment. If minimum conditions are not met, the incentive is not available for the relevant period.

## STR And ITA Differences / STR 与 ITA 的差异

中文：[[concept/special-tax-rate|特别税率 / Special Tax Rate]] 通常按年度通过年度合规报告评估。[[concept/investment-tax-allowance|投资税务津贴 / Investment Tax Allowance]] 则按每个五年 ITA 周期提交合规声明。

English: [[concept/special-tax-rate|Special Tax Rate / 特别税率]] is generally assessed annually through Annual Compliance Reports. [[concept/investment-tax-allowance|Investment Tax Allowance / 投资税务津贴]] is assessed through compliance declarations for each five-year ITA period.

## Teaching Notes / 教学提示

中文：这个机制可以帮助学生理解“批准”和“享受”不是一回事。企业先获得原则批准，但真正能享受哪一层激励，要看后续承诺履行情况。

English: This mechanism helps students understand that approval and enjoyment are not the same thing. A company first receives principle approval, but the tier it actually enjoys depends on later performance against commitments.
