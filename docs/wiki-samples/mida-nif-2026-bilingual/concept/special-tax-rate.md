# Special Tax Rate / 特别税率

## Definition / 定义

中文：特别税率（STR）是 [[entity/new-incentive-framework|NIF / 新激励框架]] 下的两种互斥税收激励之一。它为符合条件的项目在特定期间内提供较低的企业所得税税率。

English: Special Tax Rate (STR) is one of the two mutually exclusive incentives under [[entity/new-incentive-framework|NIF / 新激励框架]]. It provides a reduced corporate income tax rate for qualifying projects over a specified period.

## Incentive Range / 激励范围

| Category / 类别 | STR Range / STR 范围 |
|---|---|
| New investment / 新投资 | 0% to 10%, up to 15 years / 0% 至 10%，最长 15 年 |
| Less Developed Areas / 欠发达地区 | 0% to 15%, up to 15 years / 0% 至 15%，最长 15 年 |
| Small companies / 小型公司 | 3% to 12%, up to 15 years / 3% 至 12%，最长 15 年 |

## Compliance / 合规

中文：企业必须在拟议产品或活动开始运营前向 [[entity/malaysian-investment-development-authority|MIDA / 马来西亚投资发展局]] 提交申请。获批后，企业需要提交年度合规报告。满足最低条件可享受 Tier 2；同时满足最低条件和附加条件可享受 Tier 1；不满足最低条件则该课税年度不能享受激励。

English: The company must apply to [[entity/malaysian-investment-development-authority|MIDA / 马来西亚投资发展局]] before commencing operations for the proposed products or activities. After approval, it must submit Annual Compliance Reports. Meeting minimum conditions gives Tier 2 treatment; meeting both minimum and additional conditions gives Tier 1 treatment; failing minimum conditions removes the incentive for that year of assessment.

## Teaching Notes / 教学提示

中文：STR 适合用来讲“税率优惠如何与履约结果挂钩”。重点不是税率数字本身，而是企业每年都要证明自己仍然达到承诺。

English: STR is useful for teaching how a tax-rate benefit is linked to performance. The key point is not only the tax-rate number, but that the company must prove each year that it still meets its commitments.
