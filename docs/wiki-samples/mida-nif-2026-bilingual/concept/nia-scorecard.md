# NIA Scorecard / NIA 评分卡

## Definition / 定义

中文：NIA 评分卡是 [[entity/new-incentive-framework|新激励框架 / New Incentive Framework]] 的核心评估工具。它衡量企业承诺以及项目是否能实现国家希望看到的投资成果。

English: The NIA Scorecard is the core evaluation tool of the [[entity/new-incentive-framework|New Incentive Framework / 新激励框架]]. It measures company commitments and whether a project can deliver desired national investment outcomes.

## Pillars And Indicators / 支柱与指标

中文：制造业评分卡包括经济复杂度、高价值就业、国内联结、产业集群、包容性和可持续实践六个支柱。具体指标包括产品复杂度、研发支出占销售收入比例、技术水平、4IR 技术采用、高技能员工比例、薪资、本地投入、培训支出、校企合作、供应商发展、专利、目标行业匹配、实习与学徒、女性管理层、弱势群体就业、马来西亚员工占比和绿色制造实践。

English: The manufacturing scorecard includes six pillars: economic complexity, high-valued jobs, domestic linkages, industrial clusters, inclusivity, and sustainability practices. Indicators include product complexity, R&D expenditure as a percentage of sales revenue, technology level, 4IR adoption, high-skilled worker share, salary, local input, training expenditure, academia-industry collaboration, vendor development, patents, targeted-sector alignment, internships and apprenticeships, women in management, vulnerable-group employment, Malaysian workforce share, and green manufacturing practices.

## Role In Incentive Decision / 在激励决策中的作用

中文：评分卡结果决定项目质量，进而影响企业可获得的激励组合。评分越好，理论上对应越好的激励待遇。

English: The scorecard result determines project quality and influences the incentive package available to the company. A better score corresponds to a better incentive package.

## Teaching Notes / 教学提示

中文：NIA 评分卡可以作为“政策指标化”的例子。它把宏观政策目标转译成企业可提交、可审查、可监控的承诺。

English: The NIA Scorecard is a good example of policy operationalization. It translates macro policy goals into firm-level commitments that can be submitted, reviewed, and monitored.
