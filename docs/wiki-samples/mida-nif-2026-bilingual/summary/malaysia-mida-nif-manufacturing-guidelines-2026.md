# MIDA NIF Manufacturing Guidelines 2026 / MIDA NIF 制造业指南 2026

## Overview / 概览

中文：这份文件说明马来西亚在 [[entity/new-incentive-framework|新激励框架 / New Incentive Framework]] 下，如何为制造业新投资提供税收激励。它强调“分层”和“结果导向”：企业能拿到什么力度的激励，不只取决于行业和投资规模，也取决于项目对国家战略目标的贡献。

English: This document explains how Malaysia provides tax incentives for new manufacturing investments under the [[entity/new-incentive-framework|New Incentive Framework / 新激励框架]]. It emphasizes a tiered and outcome-based model: the incentive package depends not only on sector and investment size, but also on the project's contribution to national priorities.

## Incentive Options / 激励选项

中文：申请企业必须在两个互斥的税收激励中选择一个：[[concept/special-tax-rate|特别税率 / Special Tax Rate]] 或 [[concept/investment-tax-allowance|投资税务津贴 / Investment Tax Allowance]]。一旦申请被 [[entity/malaysian-investment-development-authority|MIDA / 马来西亚投资发展局]] 接受，选择即为最终选择。

English: Applicants must choose one of two mutually exclusive incentives: [[concept/special-tax-rate|Special Tax Rate / 特别税率]] or [[concept/investment-tax-allowance|Investment Tax Allowance / 投资税务津贴]]. Once accepted by [[entity/malaysian-investment-development-authority|MIDA / 马来西亚投资发展局]], the selection is final.

## Eligibility / 申请资格

中文：适格申请人包括根据 2016 年公司法注册并为马来西亚税务居民的公司。指南覆盖在制造业进行新投资的新公司或现有公司，并列出十五个可申请的制造业子行业。

English: Eligible applicants are companies incorporated under the Companies Act 2016 and resident in Malaysia. The guidelines cover new or existing companies undertaking new manufacturing investments and list fifteen eligible manufacturing subsectors.

## Evaluation And Compliance / 评估与合规

中文：项目会通过 [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA 评分卡 / NIA Scorecard]] 评估。评分结果影响激励包质量，后续是否持续享受优惠则取决于企业是否满足最低条件和附加条件。这一机制被称为 [[concept/tiering-approach|分层机制 / Tiering Approach]]。

English: Projects are evaluated through the [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA Scorecard / NIA 评分卡]]. The score influences the quality of the incentive package, while continued enjoyment of the incentive depends on compliance with minimum and additional conditions. This mechanism is the [[concept/tiering-approach|Tiering Approach / 分层机制]].

## Teaching Notes / 教学提示

| English Term | 中文术语 | Why It Matters / 教学重点 |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome-based incentive | 结果导向激励 | 激励不只是“给优惠”，而是绑定可衡量成果。 |
| Tiering approach | 分层机制 | 同一个激励工具下，不同履约水平对应不同待遇。 |
| NIA Scorecard | NIA 评分卡 | 把国家投资战略转成项目评估指标。 |

## Key Takeaways / 关键结论

中文：

- NIF 把制造业税收激励从静态资格审查，推进到结果导向评估。
- STR 和 ITA 是互斥选项，企业必须二选一。
- NIA 评分卡决定项目质量评价，分层机制决定激励待遇。
- 合规报告会影响企业在具体年度或具体 ITA 周期内能否享受优惠。
- 文件同时纳入了全球最低税背景，提醒大型跨国集团注意 Pillar Two 影响。

English:

- NIF shifts manufacturing tax incentives from static eligibility checks toward outcome-based evaluation.
- STR and ITA are mutually exclusive options.
- The NIA Scorecard evaluates project quality, while the tiering approach determines incentive treatment.
- Compliance reporting affects whether a company enjoys the incentive in a given year or ITA period.
- The document also includes Global Minimum Tax context for large multinational groups.
