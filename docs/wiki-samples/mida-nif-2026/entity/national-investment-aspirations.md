# National Investment Aspirations

National Investment Aspirations (NIA) is one of the two cornerstone national strategies guiding [[entity/new-incentive-framework|NIF]], alongside [[entity/new-industrial-master-plan-2030|NIMP 2030]].

In the guidelines, NIA supplies the policy basis for the [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA Scorecard]], which measures a company's commitments and expected outcomes against national priorities.

## Scorecard Connection

For manufacturing projects, the NIA Scorecard parameters are organized around pillars including:

- Increasing economic complexity.
- Creating high-valued job opportunities.
- Extending domestic linkages.
- Developing new and existing industrial clusters.
- Improving inclusivity.
- Enhancing sustainability practices.

These pillars connect project evaluation to the outcome-based design of [[entity/new-incentive-framework|NIF]].
