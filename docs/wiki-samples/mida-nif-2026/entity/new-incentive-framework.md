# New Incentive Framework

The New Incentive Framework (NIF) is introduced through Malaysia's National Budget 2026 as a phased implementation for tax incentives. In the manufacturing guidelines, NIF represents a shift to a tiered and outcome-based model.

NIF is aligned with [[entity/national-investment-aspirations|National Investment Aspirations]] and [[entity/new-industrial-master-plan-2030|New Industrial Master Plan 2030]]. Its stated objective is to attract high-growth and high-value investments, strengthen economic resilience, and connect incentives to measurable outcomes.

## Manufacturing Incentive Scope

For new manufacturing investments, NIF offers two mutually exclusive incentive types:

- [[concept/special-tax-rate|Special Tax Rate]]
- [[concept/investment-tax-allowance|Investment Tax Allowance]]

Applicants are required to select one incentive for the qualifying project. The selection is final once the application is accepted by [[entity/malaysian-investment-development-authority|MIDA]].

## Outcome-Based Structure

NIF uses the [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA Scorecard]] to evaluate project commitments and expected national outcomes. The [[concept/tiering-approach|tiering approach]] determines incentive quality based on scorecard results and later compliance with minimum and additional conditions.
