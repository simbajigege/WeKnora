# New Industrial Master Plan 2030

The New Industrial Master Plan 2030 (NIMP 2030) is referenced as a cornerstone policy blueprint guiding [[entity/new-incentive-framework|NIF]].

The guidelines use NIMP 2030 together with [[entity/national-investment-aspirations|NIA]] to frame which investments are strategically valuable. The [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA Scorecard]] includes an indicator for whether a product is within targeted sectors under NIA or core sectors under NIMP 2030.

## Manufacturing Sector Alignment

The manufacturing guidelines list fifteen eligible subsectors. These subsectors form the practical application scope for NIF manufacturing incentives and help determine whether a project aligns with targeted industrial priorities.
