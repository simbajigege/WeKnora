# Malaysian Investment Development Authority

The Malaysian Investment Development Authority (MIDA) is the agency named in the guidelines as the recipient and administrator of applications under [[entity/new-incentive-framework|NIF]] manufacturing incentives.

MIDA receives applications through the InvestMalaysia online platform. Applications with incomplete information are not accepted and are returned to the applicant company.

## Role In Approval

After evaluation using the [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA Scorecard]], MIDA issues a principle approval letter for approved projects. The letter identifies the approved tax incentive, the tiering approach, and the outcome-based conditions imposed for each tier.

## Role In Compliance

For [[concept/special-tax-rate|Special Tax Rate]], companies must submit Annual Compliance Reports to MIDA within seven months after the end of each year of assessment.

For [[concept/investment-tax-allowance|Investment Tax Allowance]], companies must submit externally verified compliance declarations to MIDA within seven months after the expiry of each five-year ITA period.
