SUMMARY: Special Tax Rate is a NIF incentive that reduces corporate income tax for qualifying projects for up to 15 years, subject to annual compliance.

# Special Tax Rate

Special Tax Rate (STR) is one of the two mutually exclusive tax incentives available under [[entity/new-incentive-framework|NIF]] for new manufacturing investments.

STR gives a reduced corporate income tax rate on a company's taxable income for a specified period. Accumulated losses incurred during the STR period can be carried forward for seven consecutive years and deducted from post-incentive income.

## Incentive Ranges

The guideline lists these STR ranges:

- New investment: 0% to 10% for up to 15 years.
- Less Developed Areas: 0% to 15% for up to 15 years.
- Small companies: 3% to 12% for up to 15 years.

## Mechanism

The company must submit its application to [[entity/malaysian-investment-development-authority|MIDA]] before commencing operations for the proposed products or activities. Commencement is defined as the date of the first sales invoice.

The company must apply for determination of the commencement year of assessment within 24 months from the principle approval letter. Failure to do so automatically cancels the principle approval letter.

Annual Compliance Reports are due to MIDA within seven months after the end of each year of assessment. Meeting minimum conditions gives Tier 2 treatment; meeting both minimum and additional conditions gives Tier 1 treatment. Failure to meet minimum conditions removes the incentive for that year of assessment.
