# Investment Tax Allowance

Investment Tax Allowance (ITA) is a capital expenditure-based incentive under [[entity/new-incentive-framework|NIF]]. It allows a company to offset a percentage of qualifying capital expenditure against statutory income.

The allowance is granted on qualifying capital expenditure incurred within a specified period. Any unutilised allowance can be carried forward to subsequent years until fully utilised.

## Incentive Range

For new manufacturing investment, the guidelines state ITA of up to 100% for a period of up to 15 years. The allowance can be used to offset between 70% and 100% of statutory income.

## Mechanism

The company must apply to [[entity/malaysian-investment-development-authority|MIDA]] before commencing operations. It must submit an application for determination of the tax incentive commencement date and a minimum-condition compliance declaration within 36 months from the principle approval letter.

The commencement date is based on the date of the first qualifying capital expenditure incurred for the approved products or activities, one day after application submission.

Compliance declarations for all minimum and additional conditions must be verified by external auditors and submitted to MIDA within seven months after the expiry of each five-year ITA period.
