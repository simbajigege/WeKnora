# NIA Scorecard

The NIA Scorecard is the core evaluation tool for [[entity/new-incentive-framework|NIF]] manufacturing incentives. It measures a company's commitments and potential to deliver desired outcomes based on criteria derived from [[entity/national-investment-aspirations|NIA]] pillars.

## Manufacturing Pillars

The manufacturing scorecard in Appendix II includes six pillars:

- Increase Economic Complexity.
- Create High Valued Job Opportunities.
- Extend Domestic Linkages.
- Develop New and Existing Industrial Cluster.
- Improve Inclusivity.
- Enhance Sustainability Practices.

## Example Indicators

The scorecard includes indicators such as product complexity, R&D expenditure as a percentage of sales revenue, technology level, 4IR adoption, percentage of high-skilled workers, median salary, local input percentage, training expenditure, academia-industry collaboration, vendor development, patents, targeted-sector alignment, internships, women in top management, vulnerable-group employment, Malaysian workforce share, and sustainability practices.

The scorecard result determines the quality of the project and influences the incentive package under the [[concept/tiering-approach|tiering approach]].
