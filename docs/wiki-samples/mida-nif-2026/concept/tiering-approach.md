# Tiering Approach

The tiering approach is the mechanism that links [[concept/nia-scorecard|NIA Scorecard]] results to incentive quality under [[entity/new-incentive-framework|NIF]].

The guidelines state that a better score corresponds to a better quantum of incentive package. Approval is conditional on the company's proposed commitments to deliver desired outcomes.

## Tier 1 And Tier 2

For [[concept/special-tax-rate|Special Tax Rate]], compliance is checked annually through the Annual Compliance Report. Meeting minimum conditions gives Tier 2 treatment for the relevant year of assessment. Meeting both minimum and additional conditions gives Tier 1 treatment.

For [[concept/investment-tax-allowance|Investment Tax Allowance]], compliance is checked for each five-year ITA period. Meeting minimum conditions gives Tier 2 treatment. Meeting both minimum and additional conditions gives Tier 1 treatment.

If the company fails to meet minimum conditions for STR, it cannot claim the incentive for that year of assessment and is taxed at prevailing rates.
