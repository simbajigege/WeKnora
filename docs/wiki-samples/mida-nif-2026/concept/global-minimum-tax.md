SUMMARY: Global Minimum Tax is Malaysia's 2025 implementation of Pillar Two rules affecting groups with annual global revenue of at least EUR 750 million.

# Global Minimum Tax

Global Minimum Tax (GMT) appears in the guidelines as Malaysia's implementation of international Pillar Two rules. The document explains Pillar Two as introducing a global minimum corporate tax rate that countries can use to protect tax bases.

The Global Anti-Base Erosion (GloBE) Rules set out the scope and mechanism of a new global minimum effective tax rate of 15%. A top-up tax is charged when a group's effective tax rate in a jurisdiction falls below 15%.

## Malaysia Implementation

Malaysia implements GMT starting from 2025, following National Budget 2024. Companies with annual global revenue of at least EUR 750 million can be subject to top-up tax if the effective tax rate in Malaysia is below 15%.

The document states that this top-up tax can be collected through the Domestic Top Up Tax mechanism under Part XI of the Income Tax Act 1967, enforced on 1 January 2025.
